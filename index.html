About Madrasa Tahfizul Quran

Established in 1989 in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, Madrasa Tahfizul Quran was founded by Abdul Bari Siddique with a vision of promoting Islamic education. The institution currently serves approximately 150 students.

Founder: Abdul Bari Siddique
